# Code of Conduct

Be respectful, constructive, and focused on the project. Harassment, discrimination, threats,
spam, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable.

Blacklist discussions can affect legitimate services. Make claims in good faith, provide
verifiable evidence, disclose relevant conflicts of interest, and avoid posting personal email
addresses or credentials.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or participation that violate these
expectations. Report conduct or security-sensitive concerns privately through a GitHub security
advisory. Maintainers will review reports fairly and protect reporter privacy where possible.
